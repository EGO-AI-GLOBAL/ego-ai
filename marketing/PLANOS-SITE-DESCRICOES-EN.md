## Team plans (shared calendars included)

Every team plan includes **everything in the matching individual tier per seat**, plus:
- **Unlimited shared calendars** (create as many as you need)
- Invite by **email** (each person must have an EGO-AI account)
- **Up to N members** (10, 20, 30, 40, 50, or 100)
- **20% cheaper** than buying N separate individual subscriptions
- Members **see and schedule** group meetings on shared calendars
- Personal reminders: alerts **1 hour, 30 minutes, and 10 minutes** before events
